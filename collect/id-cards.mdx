Guests upload a photo of their government-issued ID directly in WhatsApp — no forms, no separate portal. You can then share the collected IDs with your venue security team or hotel reception ahead of check-in.

This is useful for destination weddings, banquet venues with security check-in requirements, and any event where you need to verify guest identity in advance.

## How it works

You send guests a WhatsApp message asking them to reply with a photo of their ID. The guest takes a photo of their Aadhaar card, passport, driver's licence, or any other valid government document and sends it in the chat. The upload is stored against their guest profile in Weddingkart, and AI reads the card for the holder's name, ID number, and date of birth.

Your team can then export all collected IDs via Google Sheets and share the sheet with venue security or hotel reception staff.

## Request ID cards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Announcements">
    Open the Weddingkart app and tap **Announcements** in the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new announcement">
    Tap **Add Announcement** → **Send a ready-made message**, and pick a template from the **ID Collection** category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the message">
    The template asks guests to send a photo of their ID in the WhatsApp reply. You can optionally personalise the message before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select recipients">
    Choose the guests or groups who need to submit an ID. You can target all guests or a specific group, such as outstation guests or hotel guests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send** to deliver the request immediately, or schedule it for a later date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View collected IDs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open ID Cards Management">
    On the **Wedding Homepage**, tap **ID Cards** in the progress overview. The screen shows whether collection is **Live**, how many guests you've asked, and the share of IDs received. Tap **Received** or **Pending** to see those guests, or **View All** under Recent ID Cards to search every upload by name, phone, or file ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export all IDs to Google Sheets">
    Tap **Export ID Cards** to open **Export Guest Data**, then tap **Sync Now** under Google Sheets to export all collected ID data to your linked Google Sheet. To copy the original ID files into a Google Drive folder, use the **Google Drive** section on the same screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with your venue team">
    Share the Google Sheet with your venue security team or hotel reception. As more guests upload their IDs, the sheet updates automatically, so staff always see the latest submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Front and back

Each ID card is tagged **Front**, **Back**, or **Front + Back**, based on what the AI could read on it. If a guest sends the front and back as two separate photos, the AI recognises them as a pair and stitches them into a single PDF, marked **Combined by AI**. Tap any card to check exactly what the guest sent.

### Upload an ID on a guest's behalf

Got an ID by email or at the desk? Open the guest from your guest list and use **Upload** in the **ID Cards** section. The file is read the same way as one sent on WhatsApp — the details appear a few seconds later.

## Check who has the right documents

Tap **ID Requirements & Status** on the ID Cards screen to track not just *whether* guests sent an ID, but whether it's the *right* one.

- **Settings** — turn on **Enable ID requirements** and create requirement profiles, for example "Domestic" accepting Aadhaar or "Foreign" requiring a passport plus a visa. Mark one profile as the **Default**, or turn on **Accept any document** if any ID will do. Guests can still send any ID — nothing is auto-rejected.
- **Status** — see each guest's status (**Complete**, **Partial**, **Wrong type**, **Missing**, **Needs review**) and filter by it. Tap **Request** to message all guests with a given status asking for the correct document. Guests you can't reach right now are skipped automatically.

## Send reminders

To follow up with guests who haven't uploaded their ID, use the auto-created **ID Cards: Not Sent** group. This group contains only guests who haven't submitted yet, so confirmed guests are not disturbed.

You can resend the ID collection announcement directly to this group. If a guest uploads the wrong document, they can send a replacement at any time and their profile will update.

## Privacy and security

ID data is stored securely and is only accessible to team members on your Weddingkart account. Guests upload directly to your wedding's private channel — their documents are not shared with any third party outside of the team members you give access to.

<Note>
  ID card collection is available in the **Standard plan** only.
</Note>

## Best practices

- Request ID cards at least 1–2 weeks before the wedding to avoid last-minute submissions.
- Share the Google Sheet with your hotel reception or venue security team in advance, so they have time to process the IDs before check-in.
- Regularly sync the Google Sheet to ensure it reflects the latest uploads.
