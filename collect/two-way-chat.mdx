---
title: "Two-Way Chat"
description: "Reply to individual guest messages directly from the Weddingkart app."
---

When a guest replies to any WhatsApp message from your wedding channel, their message appears in your Weddingkart inbox. You can respond from the app, and the guest receives your reply as a WhatsApp message — without leaving Weddingkart.

Guests always stay on WhatsApp. Only you and your team operate from the Weddingkart app.

## How it works

1. You send a WhatsApp announcement to your guests (an invite, RSVP request, or event update).
2. A guest types a reply — for example, "What time should I arrive?" or "Can I bring a +1?"
3. Their message appears in your **Inbox** inside Weddingkart.
4. You type a reply in the app.
5. The guest receives your message on WhatsApp.

The conversation continues back and forth with no friction on either side.

## The inbox

Tap **Chat** in the bottom bar to open your **Team Inbox** — the list of every guest you have a conversation with for this wedding. Use the **Search by name or phone number** box at the top to find a guest. Before any guest has replied, the screen reads **Your Team Inbox is empty**.

Everyone on the wedding team shares the same inbox, and new guest messages arrive live for everyone. Saving quick replies and sending attachments need **Admin** or **Member** access (see [Team collaboration](/account/team-collaboration)). Guests never see the inbox — they only ever use WhatsApp.

If the bar at the top says **Reconnecting to chat**, the app has lost its live connection and is getting it back; **You are now connected.** means it's live again.

### Filter chips

Three chips sit above the list: **All**, **Unread (n)**, and **Needs attention (n)**. Tap **Unread** to see only conversations with messages you haven't read yet. **Needs attention** shows chats the WhatsApp assistant has handed to you (see [Automatic assistant replies](#automatic-assistant-replies)). If a filter has nothing to show, you see **You're all caught up** with *No unread chats. Switch to "All" to see every conversation.* (or *No guests currently need bot escalation.* on Needs attention).

### Pin a chat

Long-press any conversation and choose **Pin chat**. Pinned chats float to the top of the list with a 📌 next to the guest's name and a soft amber tint behind the row. Use it for the chats you want to come back to all day — your decorator, the bride's father, the venue contact. Unpin from the same long-press menu (**Unpin chat**).

### Mute a chat

Long-press a conversation and choose **Mute chat** (the menu notes *Mute is local to this device for now*). Muted chats show a 🔕 next to the guest's name and stop showing new-message notifications while the Weddingkart app is open. When the app is closed or in the background, your phone may still show them. Muted chats still appear in the inbox and keep collecting messages. Useful for talkative friends threads while you focus on logistics.

<Note>
  Pinning and muting are **per-device** and **per-wedding** — each team member curates their own inbox. If you sign in to a second phone, the pins and mutes don't follow you.
</Note>

## Notifications

Every team member on the wedding gets a push notification on each device they're signed in on when a guest sends a message. The title shows the guest's WhatsApp name and the couple's names (for example *Priya: Rahul & Anjali*). The body shows the text, or a label such as **📷 [Image]**, **📄 [Document]**, **🎤 [Voice Message]**, **🎥 [Video]**, or **📍 [Location]**. Tap the notification to open that guest's chat directly.

Notifications need to be allowed for Weddingkart in your phone's settings. A title ending in **(Previously Held)** means the message was held until the guest picked your wedding (see [Guests in more than one wedding](#guests-in-more-than-one-wedding)).

## Inside a chat

Open any guest conversation and the chat screen gives you a few shortcuts on top of the message thread.

### Quick-jump chips

Three icon chips sit in the header of every chat — **RSVP**, **Travel**, and **ID Cards**. Tap one to jump straight to that guest's submissions for that screen, without backing out and renavigating.

### Scroll-to-latest button

When you scroll up through history, a small round button appears in the bottom-right. Tap it to glide back down to the newest message. It only shows up once you're more than a screenful away from the bottom, so it stays out of your way the rest of the time.

### Bulk select

Long-press any message, then tap **Select**. The header swaps into a selection bar that shows "**N selected**" and three bulk actions:

| Action          | What it does                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Copy**        | Copies the text of every selected message to your clipboard, in chronological order.                                                                    |
| **Forward**     | Opens the standard forward flow with all selected messages.                                                                                             |
| **Share files** | Visible only when **every** selected message has media. Downloads each file and hands the whole batch to your phone's native share sheet — handy for forwarding a stack of tickets or ID cards to WhatsApp or Drive in one action. |

Tap any other message to add or remove it from the selection. Tap the **✕** in the header (or the back button) to exit selection mode.

### Replying and the 24-hour window

WhatsApp lets you reply freely for 24 hours after the guest's last message. Every new message from the guest restarts the 24 hours; your own replies don't. While that window is open, the message box shows **Replies here are free — no credits used.** and gives you:

- **📎 Attach** — send an **Image**, **Video**, **Audio**, or **Document**. Images and videos come from your gallery. Formats WhatsApp accepts: images JPG, PNG, WEBP; video MP4, 3GP, MOV; audio MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, AMR (the audio picker only offers these); documents PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLSX. Other file types may be rejected by WhatsApp.
- **/ Quick replies** — pick a saved reply to drop into the message box, where you can edit it before sending. Tap **Manage** to add, edit, or delete saved replies (each has a **Title** and a **Message**). Quick replies are shared by the whole team on this wedding and don't carry over to other weddings. They're inserted exactly as written — there are no automatic placeholders such as the guest's name.
- **🎤 Voice note** — tap the mic to record, then send (or tap the bin to discard).
- **Emoji** picker.

A "Replying to" line above the box shows which of the guest's numbers your reply will go to. If a guest has more than one number, their messages share one chat, and your reply goes to whichever number wrote last.

Once the window has expired, a banner reads **24-hour window expired. Only text messages can be sent.** Attachments, quick replies, and voice notes are hidden until the guest writes again; plain text still goes through, but WhatsApp requires it to go out in a standard reply format, so any line breaks are joined into one line. Plain-text replies typed in the chat don't use credits; sending an announcement with the megaphone does.

<Warning>
  If your wedding sends from [your own WhatsApp number](/account/own-whatsapp-number), a text reply after the window has closed may not be delivered — it stays with a clock icon. Use the megaphone below to send an announcement instead.
</Warning>

If sending media fails, you'll see a short message explaining why:

- **Couldn't send that image. Please try again.** (or video, audio, document) — the upload didn't go through; check your connection and retry. **Couldn't read that file.** or **Couldn't open that document.** means the picked file couldn't be loaded — pick it again or try another file.
- **Microphone permission is needed to record a voice message.** — allow microphone access for Weddingkart in your phone settings. **That recording was too short. Hold on a moment before sending.** — record for a little longer.

### Send an announcement from the chat

Tap the **📣 megaphone** next to the message box to send one of your existing announcements to just this guest — without leaving the conversation.

1. Tap the megaphone. A sheet lists every announcement on this wedding, with a search box.
2. Tap the one you want. The confirm step shows a preview of the message as this guest will receive it (tap **Full preview** to read all of it), plus a reminder that it uses credits and can't be undone.
3. Tap **Send**.

The message goes to **this guest only**. It shows up in the conversation immediately with a clock icon — queued, but not yet handed to WhatsApp — and reaches the guest about half a minute later, because it goes through the same scheduler that sends your other announcements rather than being sent on the spot. The clock gives way to the delivered message once it's gone.

It follows exactly the same rules as sending from the Announcements screen: credits, the 24-hour window, and delivery reporting all behave identically, and the send appears in that announcement's report alongside everything else.

<Note>
  Picking an announcement here **never changes who that announcement is addressed to**. The groups or guests you set up on the Announcements screen stay exactly as you left them, so your next bulk send is unaffected.

  Each quick-send appears as its own one-guest entry in that announcement's schedule list, which doubles as a record of who you sent it to individually.
</Note>

Announcements that aren't ready to send yet (missing a template or a required attachment, for example) appear greyed out with the reason, so you can see at a glance which ones are usable.

If a chat isn't linked to a guest on your list (someone messaged your number who isn't a guest), the megaphone shows a notice instead — there's no guest to send the announcement to.

<Tip>
  The megaphone stays available even after the 24-hour reply window closes. That's the point — once the window has expired you can only type plain text in the chat, so sending a full announcement (with its attachment and buttons) is often the fastest way to restart a conversation.
</Tip>

## Message bubbles

Bubbles in two-way chat behave a lot like WhatsApp, with a few additions tuned for wedding ops.

### Images

Tap an image to open the full-screen viewer. The viewer's built-in **Share** button lets you forward the image to anyone — Drive, the decorator's WhatsApp, your photographer.

### Documents

- **PDFs** open inside the app in the bundled PDF viewer — no leaving Weddingkart to read a ticket or invitation proof. On the web app, PDFs open in a new browser tab instead.
- **Other formats** (xlsx, docx, and so on) hand off to your phone's default app for that file type.
- If the sender didn't name the file, the bubble falls back to the filename from the URL instead of showing a bare "Document".
- Each document bubble shows the **file size** next to the filename, so you can spot the 30 MB PDF before tapping.

### Voice notes

Voice messages have an **inline player** — play and pause, scrub via the slider, and read the duration right inside the bubble. No external player needed.

### Videos

Video bubbles show a **first-frame thumbnail** instead of a grey placeholder, so you can recognise a clip at a glance.

### Link previews

When a message contains a URL, an Open-Graph card renders under the text with the page title, description, and thumbnail. Quietly skipped if the site doesn't expose preview data.

### Markdown and smart links

Messages preserve WhatsApp-style formatting (`*bold*`, `_italic_`, `~strike~`, monospace, highlight). On top of that:

- **URLs** become tappable links.
- **Indian phone numbers** (+91-style) become tappable too — tap to call or open in the dialer.

## Message tools

**Long-press a message** to open the action strip — a horizontal row of icons across the bottom of the screen:

- **Copy** — copies the message text to your clipboard.
- **Forward** — opens the forward flow for that single message.
- **Share file** — only on media messages. Downloads the bytes and hands them to your phone's native share sheet, so the receiving app gets a real file (not just a URL).
- **Select** — enters bulk-select mode with that message pre-checked.

**Tap the timestamp row** on any message (the small "10:42 ✓✓" line under the bubble) to open the **Message info** sheet. It shows the full timestamp and a plain-English delivery status — "Read by the guest", "Delivered to the guest's phone", "Sent — delivered to WhatsApp servers", "Pending — not yet sent to WhatsApp" — so you don't have to remember which tick means what.

## Messages from people not on your guest list

A chat only appears in your inbox when the sender's number matches a guest on this wedding. A message from a number that isn't on your guest list does not show up in the inbox:

- **On Weddingkart's shared number**, the sender gets no automatic reply (so an invited guest is never told they aren't invited), and the message is passed to the Weddingkart team to follow up.
- **On your own WhatsApp number**, the sender gets: *"Thanks for your message — we've passed it along to the team, they'll get back to you shortly."* The number's owners and collaborators are alerted by push notification and on WhatsApp.

To chat with that person from the inbox, [add them as a guest](/guests/adding-guests) with the exact number they messaged from; their later messages then appear in the inbox. Guests of a wedding that hasn't gone live yet are treated the same way, so make sure your wedding is live before guests start replying.

### Guests in more than one wedding

If the same phone number is a guest in more than one Weddingkart wedding, WhatsApp shows them a list and asks which wedding they're messaging about. Their messages are held until they pick, then appear in the chosen wedding's inbox. The guest can reply **RESET** at any time to pick a different wedding.

## Automatic assistant replies

On weddings where Weddingkart's WhatsApp assistant is turned on, it can answer common guest questions automatically. When it can't help, it flags the chat **Needs attention** (an amber label and left border in the inbox) and the team gets a push titled **Needs your attention** — *"✨ Neeti needs your help replying to …"*.

Inside the chat, a **Bot ON** chip in the header controls the assistant for that one guest. Tap it and choose **Pause bot until end of day** or **Pause bot until tomorrow morning**. While paused, the chip reads **Bot OFF** and a banner says *Bot paused until … — tap to release*; tap the banner or choose **Resume bot for this conversation** to turn it back on. The chip is hidden on weddings where the assistant isn't on.

## Web vs mobile

Two-way chat works in the web app and the mobile apps, with a few differences on web:

- **PDFs** open in a new browser tab rather than the in-app viewer.
- **Voice notes** need a browser that can record WhatsApp-compatible audio. If yours can't, you'll see *"This browser can't record a WhatsApp-compatible voice message. Try Chrome or Safari, or use the mobile app."* If the browser blocks the microphone: *"Couldn't access the microphone. Check the mic permission for this site in your browser and try again."*

## Key features

- **Real-time messages** — all team members on your wedding account see incoming guest messages as they arrive.
- **Per-guest conversations** — messages are organised by guest, so you can see the full thread with each person.
- **Pin and mute** — curate your inbox per device.
- **Unread filter** — one tap to focus on conversations that still need a reply.
- **Bulk actions** — copy, forward, or batch-share files across multiple messages.
- **Quick jump** — jump from a guest chat to their RSVP, Travel, or ID Card submission with one tap.
- **Announcement quick-send** — send an existing announcement to the guest you are chatting with, in three taps, without leaving the thread.
- **Rich media** — in-app image viewer, PDF reader, inline voice player, video thumbnails, link previews.
- **Smart links** — URLs and phone numbers in messages are tappable.
- **Message history** — conversations are retained for the duration of the wedding.

## Use cases

Two-way chat handles individual guest needs that bulk announcements can't address:

- Answering questions about venue address, event timing, or parking
- Following up personally with guests who haven't RSVPed
- Coordinating accommodation or transport with specific guests
- Responding to guests with special requests or dietary requirements

<Tip>
  Two-way chat works alongside bulk announcements — broadcast the same message to all guests and then handle individual follow-up questions in the inbox without switching tools.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Two-way chat is included in the **Standard plan**. It is not available in the Basic plan.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a guest's message not in my inbox?">
    Their number probably doesn't match a guest on this wedding (for example, they wrote from a different phone), or the wedding isn't live yet. Add or correct the guest's number — new messages from them will then appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does replying in chat cost credits?">
    No. Text, attachments, and voice notes you send from the chat don't use credits. Sending an announcement with the 📣 megaphone does, just like on the Announcements screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the attach, quick reply, and mic buttons disappear?">
    The guest's 24-hour window has expired. You can still send plain text, or use the megaphone to send an announcement. The buttons come back as soon as the guest writes again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are quick replies private to me?">
    No. Quick replies are saved per wedding and shared with every Admin and Member on it. They aren't copied to your other weddings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I put the guest's name in a quick reply automatically?">
    No. Quick replies are inserted exactly as saved. You can edit the text in the message box before tapping send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I muted a chat but still got a notification.">
    Mute hides notifications while the app is open. When the app is closed, your phone may still show them. Mute is also per device, so muting on one phone doesn't affect another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the guest see who on my team replied?">
    No. Every reply reaches the guest on WhatsApp from your wedding's number, whoever on the team sent it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a file size limit for attachments?">
    Weddingkart doesn't set its own limit, but WhatsApp does, and a very large file may fail to send. If you see "Couldn't send that …", try a smaller file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Weddingkart create a WhatsApp group, or a group where only we can post?">
    No. Weddingkart doesn't create or post to WhatsApp groups. Each guest gets their own personal message, and their replies come only to your team's inbox — other guests never see them. That gives you what a "no-post" group does: everyone gets your updates, nobody's replies reach the other guests, and you don't have to add guests to a group from a personal number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send to the broadcast lists in my WhatsApp Business app?">
    No. Weddingkart sends to the guests on your Weddingkart guest list, not to lists inside the WhatsApp Business app. Add those contacts as guests — pick them with **Phone Contacts** in the mobile app, or import them from Excel — and use groups instead of broadcast lists.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
