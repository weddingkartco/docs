If you send from your own WhatsApp number, every message template goes to WhatsApp for review first. WhatsApp puts each template in one of two groups:

- **Standard updates:** reminders, confirmations and logistics. These are cheap to send and almost always delivered.
- **Marketing:** anything that reads like an advert. It costs about 7× more per message, and about half of these messages don't reach guests, because WhatsApp limits how many marketing messages each person receives.

The wording decides which group a template lands in. **Proven templates** give you wording that WhatsApp has already accepted as a standard update, so you don't have to find out by trial and error.

<Note>
Proven templates are for weddings and events that send from their **own WhatsApp number**. On the shared Weddingkart number, the templates are already set up for you.
</Note>

## Where to find them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your WhatsApp templates">
    Go to your WhatsApp number's templates screen. It has two tabs: **Proven** and **My templates**. If your number has fewer than three approved templates, it opens on **Proven**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a kind of message">
    Use the row of chips to filter: **RSVP**, **On the day**, **Documents** and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Use this">
    The composer opens with the wording filled in and a live preview at the top. Below the preview, fill in each value, such as **Guest name**, **Event** or **Venue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit**. We name the template for you. Review usually takes under 15 minutes, and we'll WhatsApp you when it's done. Until then it shows as **Pending** under **My templates**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also tap **+** on the templates screen and choose **Start from proven wording**.

## What the labels mean

Each card says how much we know about its wording:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pre-approved by WhatsApp** | WhatsApp's own wording, from its template library. |
| **Adapted from wording WhatsApp approved as a standard update** | This wording was approved as a standard update on a real account. |
| **Not yet tested with WhatsApp** | Written the same way as the proven ones, but not yet submitted. It's a strong draft, not a guarantee. |

WhatsApp reviews every number separately, so even proven wording can occasionally be classed differently on your account.

## Tips

- **Change the values, not the wording.** If you edit the fixed text, you'll see **Changed from proven wording**. The more you change, the more likely WhatsApp is to class it as marketing.
- **Already on your number?** If a template you already have uses the same wording, the card shows **Use in announcement** instead, so you don't create a duplicate. To add a second copy on purpose (for another function, say), use **Add another copy** from the card's menu.
- **Read "Why it works".** Proven wording names the event and gives facts on their own lines, such as date, time and venue. It ends with something practical like "Reply if you need directions", not "Can't wait to see you!". Follow the same pattern and your own templates will pass too.
- **English only for now.**

## If you can't submit

- **"Connect your WhatsApp number to use proven templates."** Your wedding sends from the shared Weddingkart number, or your number is disconnected. Connect your own number to add templates.
- **"Only people who manage this WhatsApp number can add templates."** You can read the cards, but only the number's owner and the people they've given access to can add templates. Ask the person named in the message to give you access. See [Team collaboration](/account/team-collaboration).

## If WhatsApp classed your template as marketing

This isn't something you did wrong. The template card shows the proven wording for the same kind of message. Tap **Switch to wording WhatsApp approves** to submit it as a new template, with your values carried over. Your old template stays until the new one is approved.

## Invitations and save-the-dates

Proven templates cover everyday updates only. Invitations, save-the-dates and wording in your own style aren't in the library, because that wording is the most likely to be classed as marketing and it's worth getting right.

For those, tap **Contact support** on the **Proven** tab and we'll connect you with a partner who writes custom templates for you.
