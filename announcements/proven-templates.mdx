title: "Start from a proven template"
description: Use wording WhatsApp has already accepted as a standard update, so your template is approved and delivered
---

If you send from your own WhatsApp number, every message template goes to WhatsApp for review first. WhatsApp puts each template in one of two groups:

- **Standard updates:** reminders, confirmations and logistics. These are cheap to send and almost always delivered.
- **Marketing:** anything that reads like an advert. About half of these messages don't reach guests, because WhatsApp limits how many marketing messages each person receives.

The wording decides which group a template lands in. The **Catalog** gives you starting wording that WhatsApp has usually approved as a standard update, so you don't have to find out by trial and error. WhatsApp makes the final call on every template.

<Note>
The Catalog is for weddings and events that send from their **own WhatsApp number**. On the shared Weddingkart number, the templates are already set up for you.
</Note>

## Where to find them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Templates page">
    On the web app, open your event, tap **+** on Announcements and choose **Send a ready-made message**. The **Templates** page has two tabs: **My templates** and **Catalog**. If your number is new, it opens on **Catalog**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a kind of message">
    Search, or use the row of chips to filter: **RSVP**, **On the day**, **Documents** and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Use this">
    The **New template** screen opens with the wording filled in and a live preview. Change the wording, the attachment and the buttons as you like, and write the blue parts, which every guest sees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to WhatsApp for review">
    Tap **Send to WhatsApp for review**. We name the template for you. WhatsApp usually decides within about 1 hour for businesses verified with Meta, and up to 24 hours otherwise. Until then it shows as **Pending** under **My templates**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A catalog card is only a starting point. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp until you send it for review, and WhatsApp reviews every template itself, so approval is never guaranteed.

## What the labels mean

Each card says where its wording comes from:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp's own wording** | From WhatsApp's own template library. |
| **Based on approved wording** | Adapted from wording WhatsApp approved as a standard update on a real account. |
| **New wording** | Written the same way as the others, but not yet tested with WhatsApp. It's a strong draft, not a guarantee. |

WhatsApp reviews every number separately, so even catalog wording can occasionally be classed differently on your account.

## Tips

- **Change the values, not the wording.** The more you change the fixed text, the more likely WhatsApp is to class it as marketing. The checks on the **New template** screen warn you when a change starts to read like marketing.
- **Using a card again makes another template.** The card doesn't change after you use it; each use creates a new template with the next free name. Find the ones you've made under **My templates**.
- **Follow the pattern.** Catalog wording names the event and gives facts on their own lines, such as date, time and venue. It ends with something practical like "Reply if you need directions", not "Can't wait to see you!". Follow the same pattern and your own templates will pass too.
- **English only for now.**

## If you can't submit

- **"Connect your WhatsApp number to use these"** Your wedding sends from the shared Weddingkart number, or your number is disconnected. Connect your own number to add templates.
- **"Only people who manage this WhatsApp number can add templates."** You can read the cards, but only the number's owner and the people they've given access to can add templates. Ask the person named in the message to give you access. See [Team collaboration](/account/team-collaboration).

## If WhatsApp classed your template as marketing

This isn't something you did wrong. The template shows **Moved to marketing** on the **Templates** page. It still works, but some guests won't receive it. To get a standard-update version, go to the **Catalog**, tap **Use this** on the same kind of message and send it for review as a new template.

## Invitations and save-the-dates

The Catalog covers everyday updates only. Invitations, save-the-dates and wording in your own style aren't in the library, because that wording is the most likely to be classed as marketing and it's worth getting right.

For those, write your own with **Write your own** on the **Templates** page, and use the checks on the screen to keep the wording factual. See [Create your own message templates](/announcements/own-templates).
