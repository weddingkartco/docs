</Note>

If you hire someone for a few days to phone your guests, you don't have to add them to your team or ask them to install anything. Make them a **Guest caller** field staff link. They open the link on their phone, enter a one-time code, and see a **My calls** page with only the guests on the call lists you chose.

This works like the link you may already use for a hamper runner or a pickup driver. See [Team collaboration](/account/team-collaboration#field-staff-web) for field staff in general. For how call lists work, see [Call your guests](/guests/call-guests).

## Link or team member?

| | Guest caller field staff link | Team member with the **Guest caller** job |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | A caller hired for a few days | Someone already on your team |
| Needs the Weddingkart app | No. Works in the phone's browser | Yes |
| Sign-in | One-time code the first time on a phone | Their normal Weddingkart login |
| What they see | Only the guests on the call lists you choose | The call lists they're given, in the app |
| Ends | When the wedding ends, or when you remove the link | When you change or remove their access |

To add someone to your team instead, see [Call your guests](/guests/call-guests#who-can-do-what).

## Make a Guest caller link

Only the wedding owner can add or remove field staff. Other team members see **Only the wedding owner can add field staff**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Field staff">
    In the web app, open **Team** and tap **Field staff**. The page explains: *Give a hamper runner, reception person or driver a link. They open it on their phone, no app needed. It stops working when the wedding is over, or when you remove it.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add field staff">
    Under **What will they do?** choose **Guest caller** (*Calls only the guests on their call lists*). The other choices are **Hamper runner**, **Reception desk** and **Pickup driver**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their name and phone number">
    Type their **First name** and **Phone number**. The page reminds you: *We send them a code on this number.* That code is how they sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Which call lists?">
    Tick the call lists this person should work, for example **Invite call** and **Sangeet RSVP**. The page says: *They see names and phone numbers of these guests only, never the full guest list.* Make the lists first (see [Make a call list](/guests/call-guests#make-a-call-list)); a list you haven't made can't be ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Make link">
    Copy the link and share it with the caller yourself. Weddingkart doesn't message the link for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then assign guests to the caller: open the list, tap **Assign callers**, and include the new caller. See [Assign callers](/guests/call-guests#assign-callers). If you don't assign guests to individual callers, any caller on a list can take any unassigned guest.

## What the caller sees: My calls

The caller opens the link on their phone and enters the one-time code. The first time on a phone they confirm with the code; after that the same phone stays signed in. Don't share the link with anyone else.

The **My calls** page shows:

- A greeting such as *Hi Priya · 54 to call today · 3 call backs*, with the wedding name and the role **Guest caller**.
- One tab per call list (for example *Invite call · 41* and *Sangeet RSVP · 13*) and a **Call backs** tab. Guests who asked to be called back come first.
- A card for each guest with their name, group, a hidden phone number (*+91 •••••• 3210*), their status (*Not called yet*, *AI couldn't reach*, *Call back 5:30 pm*), what to ask (*Ask: Coming? · How many? · Arrival time*) and a **Call** button.

Tapping **Call** opens the same choice as in the app: **Call from my phone**, or **Recorded call** if recorded calls are available to this link. After the call, the same **After the call** form opens, with the outcomes, questions, voice note and team note described in [Call your guests](/guests/call-guests#after-the-call-own-phone).

The caller can see:

- Names and partly hidden numbers of the guests on their lists. The full number is used only when they tap **Call**.
- The questions for each list.

The caller cannot see:

- The full guest list, other call lists, payments, announcements or chats.
- Guests on lists you didn't tick, even if they change the web address. Those requests are refused.

## Recorded calls from a caller link

A caller link can place recorded calls only when recorded calls were switched on for that link. If they weren't, the caller sees only **Call from my phone**. Recorded calls use your calling number and your wedding's credits. See [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number).

A hired caller can't play recordings. Playing recordings is a separate permission for your own team (see [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number#who-can-play-recordings)).

## How a caller's work is recorded

- Each call card in the guest's chat shows who made the call, for example *By Priya*.
- For calls made from the caller's own phone, Weddingkart can't hear the call. It only knows what the caller enters in the **After the call** form, so the outcome and answers are whatever the caller reported. Review answers from important calls.
- The answers they save appear on the guest's page with the **Human call** tag.
- If someone already has a guest in a call, a second caller sees a message such as *Priya is calling this guest* for up to 10 minutes.

## When the link stops working

- **The wedding ends.** The link stops working on its own.
- **You remove it.** Open **Team** › **Field staff** and tap **Remove** on the caller's card (or **Remove all field staff**). The next thing the caller does fails straight away. Anything they hadn't saved yet is lost, and the page tells them so. Answers they already saved stay on the guests.

Each caller's card shows whether the link is **Active**, **Removed** or **Ended**, and how many phones have used it.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the caller need to install Weddingkart?">
    No. They open the link in their phone's browser, enter the one-time code, and use the **My calls** page. There is no app and no password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the caller see about guests?">
    Only the guests on the call lists you ticked: names, group and a partly hidden phone number. They never see the full guest list, payments, announcements or chats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the caller use their own number or yours?">
    Both are possible. **Call from my phone** uses the caller's own phone and number, so the guest sees the caller's personal number. **Recorded call** uses your business's calling number, if recorded calls are available to that link. See [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I added the wrong call lists. Can I change them?">
    Remove the link and make a new one with the right lists ticked. The old link stops working as soon as you remove it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the caller change the guest's RSVP or details?">
    The caller can save the answers to the questions on their list and the missing details the form shows (for example RSVP, guests coming, arrival time, food). They can't edit the rest of the guest's record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't my team member make a field staff link?">
    Only the wedding owner can add or remove field staff. Ask the person who created the wedding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the caller's notes?">
    A team note a caller types is shown to your team on the call card in chat. It is never sent to the guest and never read by the WhatsApp assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the link cost anything?">
    Credits are only used when the caller does something that costs credits, such as a recorded call. The app shows that cost before the call starts, and it comes from the wedding's credits. See [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
