</Note>

A **calling number** is a phone number in your business's name that Weddingkart uses to place **recorded calls** to your guests. It is the second way to call from a call list. The first, **Call from my phone**, needs no setup (see [Call your guests](/guests/call-guests)).

## Why use a calling number?

- **Guests see your business number**, not your caller's personal number.
- **The answers fill in by themselves.** Every recorded call is turned into a summary and proposed answers on the guest's page, so your caller doesn't type them in.
- **Guests who call back reach the person who called them.** See [When a guest calls back](#when-a-guest-calls-back).
- **You keep the recording** (for the team members you allow) to check what was said.

Weddingkart can't hear a call placed from a caller's own phone, which is why recording needs a calling number. A recorded call runs through our telephony partner, **Vobiz**, who connects the caller to the guest and records the call.

| | Call from my phone | Recorded call |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | None | One-time setup (about 5 minutes) |
| Number the guest sees | The caller's own number | Your business's calling number |
| Recorded | No | Yes. The guest is told |
| Answers | The caller fills in the **After the call** form | Filled in from the recording; the caller checks them |
| Callbacks | Reach the caller's own phone | Reach the person who last called, then you |
| Cost | The app shows any cost before you start | The app shows the cost per minute before you start |

<Warning>
The calling number is registered in **your business's name**, so you are responsible for how your team uses it. Use it only to call guests of the wedding about the wedding, between 9 am and 9 pm IST, and stop calling a guest who asks you to.
</Warning>

## Who can set it up

Only the planner who owns the calling number can set it up or change it. Team members and hired callers who can't set it up see **Ask the planner to set up a calling number** instead of a **Set up** button.

A calling number belongs to you, the planner, not to one wedding. You set it up once and then switch it on for each wedding that should use it. A wedding uses at most one calling number.

## Set up your calling number

You can start from any of these places. All of them open the same set-up screens:

- **Recorded call** on the call sheet (the main one): tap **Set up calling number**.
- The **Record calls on your own number** banner on the **Call lists** screen.
- **New call list** › **Who calls?** › **Set up calling number**.
- The **Set up** line at the bottom of the **After the call** form.
- **Settings** › **Calling number**, which reads *Not set up · record guest calls on your own number*.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read what you get">
    The first screen is **Calling number** (*For all your weddings*) with the title **Record guest calls on your own number**. It lists what you get:
    - *Guests see your business number, not your caller's personal number.*
    - *Every call is recorded and turned into answers on the guest's page.*
    - *Guests who call back reach the person who called them.*

    Below that, **What you need (about 5 minutes)** lists the documents, and **Cost** shows what the number and recorded calls cost. The app shows these amounts before you go further. It also says *Calls from your own phone don't use credits.* and *The number is registered in your business's name, so you are responsible for how your team uses it. Our telephony partner Vobiz checks your documents.*

    Tap **Start verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    Enter your **business name**, choose your **business type** and give an **email address** for the verification. The business type decides which documents you need (see the table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your business">
    The screen **Verify your business** opens Vobiz's page inside the app. It asks for the documents that match your business type, then returns you to Weddingkart. While it loads you see a spinner, and after you submit you see **Checking your documents…**. When Vobiz confirms, you move on automatically.

    The verification link is valid for **7 days**. If a document is rejected, the app shows what went wrong and a **Try again** button that opens a fresh link. If you close the app part-way, open **Settings** › **Calling number** later and it picks up where you left off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your number">
    The **Pick your number** screen confirms *Business verified* with your business name and the documents checked. It says *Pick a number. Guests see it whenever your team makes a recorded call.* and lists mobile numbers; tap **Show more numbers** for more.

    Under **Use it for these weddings**, tick the weddings that should use this number. The app shows the monthly cost and says it renews monthly.

    Tap **Get** followed by the number you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the number to be ready">
    The app shows **Getting your number ready…** while the number is set up. This usually takes about 2 minutes. Tapping **Get** twice, or losing your connection part-way, never buys two numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After setup, the **Calling number** row in **Settings** reads, for example, *+91 92XXX XX418 · On for this wedding*, with an on/off switch for each wedding. Only the planner who owns the number sees the set-up options.

### Documents by business type

| Business type | What you need |
|---|---|
| **Individual** or **sole proprietor** (proprietorship) | **PAN**, and **Aadhaar through DigiLocker** |
| **Company**, **LLP** or **partnership** | **PAN**, and **GST** or **CIN** |

Your documents go to Vobiz, our telephony partner, who checks them. The documents are used to register the number in your business's name.

### Turn it on or off for a wedding

Open the wedding, go to **Settings** › **Calling number**, and use the switch. When it's off for a wedding, **Recorded call** isn't offered for that wedding, but your number stays yours and works on your other weddings.

## What guests hear

- The guest's phone shows your calling number as the caller.
- When the guest picks up, they hear: *"This call is recorded for the wedding team."* before the conversation starts.
- Your caller sees that the call is recorded on screen.

<Note>
Whether a guest's phone shows a name beside your number depends on their phone and caller-ID apps. Weddingkart shows the number you registered.
</Note>

## Place a recorded call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the call button">
    On a call list, a guest's page or a guest's chat, tap the call button. The sheet shows **Recorded call** with the number it uses (*From* your calling number) and says: *We ring your phone first, then* the guest. *The guest hears that the call is recorded.* It also says **Answers filled for you** and shows the cost per minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Recorded call">
    Tap **Recorded call**. Tick **Remember my choice for this wedding** if you want to skip the sheet next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer your phone first">
    Weddingkart rings the caller's phone first. The **Recorded call** screen shows three steps:
    1. **Your phone answered** — on the caller's number (partly hidden).
    2. **Ringing** the guest — the guest sees your calling number.
    3. **Talking, recorded** — *On pickup, the guest hears: "This call is recorded for the wedding team."*

    The screen also lists what to ask (*Invite call · ask about: RSVP, Arrival time*). The caller doesn't need to type anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the call">
    Either side can hang up. The caller can also tap **End call**. A recorded call can last up to 15 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Calling hours

Recorded calls (and AI calls) can only be placed between **9 am and 9 pm IST**. Outside those hours the app doesn't place the call and tells you so.

### What if a recorded call can't start?

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **All lines busy, try in a minute** | Only a limited number of recorded calls can run at the same time across Weddingkart, and they're all in use | Wait a minute and tap **Recorded call** again. Or use **Call from my phone** |
| A prompt to top up | The wedding doesn't have enough credits for a recorded call | Top up from **Wallet** (see [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits)). **Call from my phone** still works |
| **Set up calling number** | No calling number is ready for this wedding | Set one up, or ask the planner |
| Outside calling hours | It's before 9 am or after 9 pm IST | Try again in calling hours |
| Not allowed | Your access doesn't include recorded calls on this number | Ask the planner to give you the **Guest caller** job |

If you tap **Recorded call** twice quickly, only one call is placed. If your caller doesn't pick up, or the guest doesn't pick up, there is nothing to record, the call card says there was no answer, and the credits for that call come back.

### Credits

A recorded call uses the wedding's credits. The cost per minute is shown on the call sheet and on the **Recorded call** screen before and during the call, and the credits come out of the wedding wallet. Some credits are held when the call starts and the exact amount is settled after the call ends, so your balance can move slightly afterwards. Calls that don't connect are refunded. See [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits) and [weddingkart.co/pricing](https://www.weddingkart.co/pricing).

## After a recorded call

Within a few minutes, the call appears as a card in the guest's chat: **Human call · Answered** with the duration, who called and from which number. The card shows:

- **AI summary** — labelled *not a transcript*: a short description of what was said.
- **Chips** for the answers found, such as **RSVP · Yes**, **Guests · 2**, **Food · Veg**.
- **Listen** to play the recording, if you're allowed to (see below).
- **View transcript** to read the conversation.

Tap the card to open the **call report**. It shows the call's outcome and length, the guest's number, the languages heard, who called, the **AI summary**, the **Recording** player (with a line such as *Recorded on* your number *· kept for N days*), the **Transcript** with each speaker labelled (**Show full transcript** expands it), and the answers under **RSVP from the call**.

The answers are **proposals for you to check**. Open the call report, check each answer, and confirm. Confirmed answers are saved to the guest and shown with the **Human call** tag. Some items, such as an ID card the guest says they'll send later, are held for review rather than saved: the report lists them under **Held for review**, with a **Review** button.

Processing a recording can take a few minutes. The summary and answers appear as soon as they're ready.

## Who can play recordings

Playing a recording is a separate permission called **Play call recordings**. By default the wedding owner and co-planners have it. For any other team member, turn it on in **Change access** › **Options** › **Play call recordings**.

Without it, a team member sees the summary and answers but no **Listen** button. A hired caller on a field staff link can't play recordings.

Weddingkart keeps a record of who played which recording and when.

## How long recordings are kept

The call report shows how long a recording is kept. When that time ends, the recording and its transcript are deleted. The answers you confirmed stay on the guest's page. Notes and recordings are for your team only: the guest-facing WhatsApp assistant never sees them.

## When a guest calls back

Guests often call the number that rang them. With a calling number, those calls aren't lost.

1. The call rings the **person who last called that guest**.
2. If they don't answer, it rings **you, the planner**.
3. If nobody answers, the guest's chat gets a **Missed call from** card, for example *Called* your number *back · rang Priya, then* the planner *· nobody answered*, and your team gets a push notification.

Other cases:

- **A number that isn't one of your guests** rings the planner, and nothing is added to any guest's chat.
- **A guest who is on more than one of your weddings** rings the planner too, because Weddingkart can't tell which wedding they mean.
- **Weddingkart's AI doesn't answer callbacks.** They go to your people.
- **Calls from a caller's own phone** show the caller's personal number, so callbacks go to that phone directly, not through Weddingkart.

## If you stop using the number

If you stop using the number, ask Weddingkart support what happens to it and whether it can be released.
