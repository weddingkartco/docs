---
title: "Families"
description: Keep a household together, and add guests without a phone who are reached through one person in their family
---

A family is a household on your guest list: for example Rajesh, Sunita and their two children. One person in the family with a phone number is its **main contact**. Family members without a phone, such as small children or an elderly grandparent, can be on your guest list without a number. Messages for them go to the main contact, and the main contact answers the RSVP for the family.

Families are not [groups](/guests/guest-groups). A guest can be in many groups but in only one family. Use groups to choose who gets an announcement. Use a family to say who belongs together and who is reached through whom.

Families are internal to Weddingkart. They are not WhatsApp groups, and guests never see them.

<Note>
  Families are in the web app at [app.weddingkart.co](https://app.weddingkart.co). The iPhone and Android apps don't show them yet.
</Note>

Anyone on your team who can edit guests can create and change families. Team members who can only view guests see a guest's family but can't change it.

## How families work

- **One family per guest.** A guest is in one family only. Moving them to another family takes them out of the first.
- **The main contact has a phone.** Only a member with a phone number can be the main contact. A family has one main contact.
- **Members without a phone are reached through the main contact.** They are never sent a WhatsApp message and never called, they don't get an RSVP link of their own, and they use no credits.
- **The main contact answers for the family.** A member without a phone is never counted as "not answered" and gets no RSVP reminders: the main contact's answer covers them.
- **A guest without a phone has to be in a family.** Outside a family, every guest needs a phone number. To add someone without a phone, add them to a family.

## Where you see families

- **Guest list** — a line under each family member's name shows their family:
  - "Sharma Jaipur · 4" for a member with a phone: the family's name and how many people are in it.
  - "Sharma Jaipur · No phone · via Rajesh" for a member without a phone: Rajesh is the main contact they are reached through.
  - "Sharma Jaipur · No phone yet" when nobody in the family has a phone yet, so nobody can be reached.
- **Guest page** — the **Family** section lists every member (tap one to open their page), marks the **Main contact**, and marks anyone with **No phone**.
- **A member without a phone** — their guest page says, for example, "Messages for Aarav go to Rajesh, the family's main contact." **Open Rajesh's chat** opens the main contact's chat, and above RSVP you'll see "Rajesh answers for the family."

## Create a family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a guest's page">
    Go to **Guests** and open someone who will be in the family. The person who'll be the main contact is a good place to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add to a family">
    In the **Family** section, tap **Add to a family**. The sheet that opens is titled **Add Rajesh to a family** and reminds you "A guest is in one family only."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the family, or pick one">
    Type a **New family name** (for example "Sharma Jaipur") and tap **Create**. To add the guest to a family you've already made, find it under **Or move to a family** (use **Search families**) and tap it instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Family names

- Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter: "Sharma Jaipur" and "sharma  jaipur" are the same family.
- Two families in one wedding can't have the same name. If you try, you'll see "A family called 'Sharma' already exists. Try a different name, like 'Sharma (2)'."
- A family name can be up to 200 characters.
- Use something that tells households apart, such as surname and city ("Sharma Jaipur", "Sharma Pune").

## Add family members

**Someone new:** on any family member's page, tap **Add member** in the **Family** section. The **Add family member** sheet shows which family they're joining (for example "In Sharma Jaipur, with Rajesh") and works like adding any guest, except that:

- The phone is optional: the field is **Phone Number (optional in a family)**. Leave it empty for a child or anyone without a phone.
- The groups of the guest you started from are already ticked, so the new member is in the same groups. Change them if you need to.
- Tap **Save & Add Another** to save and add the next member straight away.

**Someone already on your list:** open their guest page, tap **Add to a family** (or **Change family** if they're in another family) and choose the family under **Or move to a family**.

## Change the main contact

Open the page of a family member who has a phone and tap **Make main contact**. You'll see "Rajesh is now the main contact". Members without a phone can't be the main contact.

If the main contact leaves the family, is deleted, or loses their phone number, another member with a phone becomes the main contact. Open the family afterwards to check who the members without a phone are now reached through.

## Move someone or take them out

On the guest's page, tap **Change family**. The sheet is titled **Change Rajesh's family**, and you can:

- **Take Rajesh out of Sharma Jaipur** — they're no longer in a family, and you'll see "Rajesh is no longer in a family".
- **New family name** → **Create** — they move into a new family.
- **Or move to a family** — they move into another existing family.

A member without a phone can't be taken out of a family, because a guest without a phone must be in one. The sheet says "Aarav has no phone, so they stay in a family. Add a number first to take them out." You can still move them to another family, or delete them like any other guest.

When the last person leaves a family, the family is removed.

## Warnings in the Family section

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| "Nobody in this family has a phone, so nobody can be messaged. Add a number to one of them." | No member has a phone, so there is no main contact and nobody in the family gets messages. | Add a phone number to one adult in the family. They become the main contact. |
| "This family has 22 people. That looks like a side, not a household." | The family has more than 15 people. A family is meant to be one household, not a whole side or clan. | Split it into households. Use a [group](/guests/guest-groups) for a side. |
| "Couldn't change the family" | The change wasn't saved. | Try again. |

## Announcements, calls and credits

When you send an announcement, the footer and **Review** show members without a phone on their own line: "2 in a family with no phone: their main contact gets it". They're counted apart from guests who simply have no phone number.

- **The main contact gets the message once**, as themselves. The member without a phone gets nothing directly, so nothing is charged for them.
- **Only when the main contact is a recipient too.** If you send to a group that has Aarav in it but not Rajesh, nobody in that family gets the message. Put the whole family in the group, or at least the main contact.
- **Calls** go to people with a phone only. A member without a phone is never called.

## RSVP

A member without a phone doesn't get an RSVP link. The main contact answers for the family, and that answer, including how many people are coming, covers everyone in it. Members without a phone are never shown as "not answered" and get no reminders.

## Families from an Excel file

You can set up families while you [import your guest list](/guests/excel-import). Add a **Family** column: every row with the same value, on any sheet in the file, is one family. Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter. A value that matches a family already in your wedding adds the row to that family.

| Name | Phone | Family | Main contact |
|------|-------|--------|--------------|
| Rajesh Sharma | +91 98765 43210 | Sharma Jaipur | Yes |
| Sunita Sharma | +91 91234 56789 | Sharma Jaipur | |
| Aarav Sharma | | Sharma Jaipur | |
| Neha Gupta | +91 99887 76655 | Gupta Delhi | |

Here Rajesh, Sunita and Aarav become the family "Sharma Jaipur" with Rajesh as the main contact, and Aarav joins without a phone. Neha becomes the family "Gupta Delhi" on her own.

### Recognised column names

Capital letters don't matter, and `_` or `:` count as spaces (so `family_name` and `Family:` work too).

| Column | Header names Weddingkart recognises |
|--------|-------------------------------------|
| Family | **Family** or **Household**, on its own or followed by Name, ID, Group, #, No, No., Number, Num or Code. For example: Family, Family Name, Family ID, Household, Household #, Household No., Family Code. |
| Main contact (optional) | Main contact, Primary member, Primary contact, Head of family, Head of the family, Head of household, Household head, H/O family, HOF, Family head. |

In the Main contact column, **Yes**, **Y**, **True**, **1**, **✓**, **✔** or **x** marks the main contact. Anything else, or an empty cell, means "not the main contact".

### Turn on Use the Family column

Weddingkart never makes families from a file unless you ask. When your file has a Family column, the upload screen shows a card after the file is read: **Use the "Family" column as families** (with your column's name), with a switch that is off to start with. The card shows what turning it on would do:

- "12 families found"
- "5 people without a phone join their family"
- A warning when a family has more than 15 people, for example: *"Shah" has 22 people. That looks like a side, not a household.* Check your Family column before you go on.

Turn the switch on, then continue with **Next: Choose Groups** as usual. With it on:

- Rows without a phone that have a Family value are imported as family members. Rows without a phone and without a Family value are skipped as before.
- The Family and Main contact columns are used for families only, so they aren't offered as groups or as per-guest info.
- A member without a phone gets the group of their sheet, like everyone else on that sheet.
- A person listed on several sheets (for example "All guests" and "Sangeet") with the same name and Family is one person, in both sheets' groups.
- When the import finishes, you'll see "12 families made or updated" and "People in a family are reached together".

With the switch off, no family is made or changed. The Family column is offered as groups (like `family=sharma jaipur`) and rows without a phone are skipped, as in any import. A file downloaded from Weddingkart is the one exception: its Family and Main contact columns are never offered as groups.

### Who becomes the main contact

- A **Yes** in the Main contact column on a row with a phone makes that person the main contact.
- A Yes on a row without a phone, or two Yeses in one family, is ignored.
- With no Yes, an existing main contact stays. A new family gets the first person with a phone, in the order of the rows in your file.

## Re-importing a file with families

You can upload an updated file at any time. With families, a re-import works like this:

- **Without the switch on, families don't change.** Families are only changed when you turn on **Use the "Family" column as families**.
- **A Family value moves a guest into that family**, and creates the family if the name is new.
- **An empty Family cell changes nothing.** To take someone out of a family, use **Change family** on their guest page.
- **The main contact changes only on a Yes.** Without one, the current main contact stays.
- **Row order doesn't matter.** Moving rows up or down, or onto another sheet, changes nothing.
- **People without a phone are matched by name inside their family.** Aarav in "Sharma Jaipur" in the file is the Aarav already in that family, so nobody is added twice.

Some rows can't be matched safely. They aren't imported, nothing is changed or removed, and they are counted among the skipped rows:

| Row | Why it's held back | What to do |
|-----|--------------------|------------|
| Two people without a phone with the same name and Family, on the same sheet | Weddingkart can't tell them apart. | Give one of them a fuller name ("Aarav Sharma" and "Aarav Sharma Jr"). |
| A person without a phone whose name is very close to someone in their family ("Arav Sharma" when "Aarav Sharma" is in the family) | It may be the same person with a typo. | Use the same spelling as in the app, or a clearly different name. |
| A person without a phone whose Family changed in the file | There's no phone to prove it's the same person, so Weddingkart won't move them or add a second copy. | Move them with **Change family** on their guest page. |

### Re-import a file you downloaded from Weddingkart

When your wedding has families, two downloads add four columns: **Guest ID**, **Family**, **Family ID** and **Main contact**:

- the guest table: **Guests** → **Table** → **Download (Excel)**;
- the [Master Sheet](/account/google-sheets-integration) in Google Sheets.

Guest ID (like `G-1234`) and Family ID (like `F-56`) tie each row to a guest and a family. You can correct the downloaded file and import it again with the switch on:

- **Keep Guest ID and Family ID as they are.** Thanks to Guest ID, you can fix the name of someone without a phone, or give them their first number, and the same guest is updated instead of a new one added.
- **To move someone to another family in the file,** change their Family and clear their Family ID. A row whose Family ID and Family don't match is held back, with a note like "Family ID F-56 is 'Sharma Jaipur' but the Family column says 'Sharma Pune'. Clear the Family ID to move this guest, or fix the Family column."
- **For a new person,** add a row and leave Guest ID and Family ID empty. Don't copy another guest's row with its Guest ID.

A file downloaded from a different wedding still works: its Guest IDs and Family IDs are ignored, and rows match by phone number as usual.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a family a WhatsApp group?">
    No. Families only organise your guest list inside Weddingkart. Guests never see them, and no WhatsApp group is created.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a family and a group?">
    A group is a list you send announcements to, and a guest can be in any number of groups. A family is one household: a guest is in one family only, and members without a phone are reached through the family's main contact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a guest be in two families?">
    No, a guest is in one family only. Adding them to a family moves them out of the one they were in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do members without a phone use credits?">
    No. They're never messaged or called, so nothing is charged for them. The main contact's message is charged as usual.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't the grandmother get the announcement?">
    A member without a phone never gets messages directly: their family's main contact does. The main contact only gets it if they're among the announcement's recipients. Check that the main contact is in the group you sent to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a guest without a phone outside a family?">
    No. Outside a family, every guest needs a phone number, because otherwise nobody could reach them. Add them to a family with someone who has a phone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete a family?">
    Move or take out everyone in it. When the last person leaves, the family is removed. Members without a phone can't be taken out on their own: move them to another family, add a number to them first, or delete them like any other guest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I rename a family?">
    Not in the app. Changing the Family value in your Excel file doesn't rename the family: people with a phone move into a new family with the new name, and rows of people without a phone are held back. Keep the family's name the same in your file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='My "Family" column just says Yes or No. Should I turn on the switch?'>
    No. A Yes/No column like that marks who is family, not which household they're in. Leave **Use the "Family" column as families** off, and the column is offered as groups (`family=yes`, `family=no`), as before.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Family column holds a number for each household. Does that work?">
    Yes. Headers like "Household #", "Family No." or "Family ID" are recognised, and every row with the same number is one family. The family is named after the number, so a name like "Sharma Jaipur" is easier to read in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A family member without a phone appears on two sheets. Will they be added twice?">
    No. With the switch on, the same name with the same Family on different sheets is one person, and they're added to both sheets' groups. Two rows with the same name and Family on the same sheet are held back, because they could be two different people.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Excel download show families?">
    Yes, when your wedding has families. The guest table download (**Download (Excel)**) and the Master Sheet add Guest ID, Family, Family ID and Main contact columns.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
