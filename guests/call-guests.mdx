<Note>
Call Guests is in **Beta**. It works end to end, and we're improving it based on what planners tell us.
</Note>

Many planners hire someone to phone every guest for an RSVP, arrival time, ID card or food choice, and then type the answers into a sheet. **Call Guests** replaces that sheet. You make a **call list** (who to call and why), choose **who calls** (your team, the AI agent, or the AI first and then your team), and every call ends as **answers on the guest's page** and a **call card in the guest's chat**.

There are two ways a person can place a call from a list:

- **Call from my phone.** The caller's own phone dials the guest. Weddingkart can't hear this call, so the caller fills in a short **After the call** form (outcomes, answers, an optional voice note). No setup needed.
- **Recorded call.** Weddingkart rings the caller first, then the guest, from your business's own calling number. The call is recorded and turned into answers automatically. This needs a one-time setup. See [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number).

<Note>
The AI agent is the same AI voice call you may already use from announcements. A call list simply lets you point it at the guests you choose, and hand the guests it couldn't reach to your team. See [Voice announcements](/announcements/voice-announcements#ai-voice-call-languages) for the languages the AI agent speaks.
</Note>

## What is a call list?

A call list is a named set of guests plus what the call is for. Each list has:

- **A name**, for example *Invite call* or *Sangeet RSVP*.
- **A purpose**, so the caller knows what to say. Choose one of **Invite to the wedding**, **Confirm RSVP**, **Travel and arrival**, **Food preference** or **Something else**.
- **What the caller should say** (a short brief) and **Questions to ask**. Each question saves its answer to the guest automatically.
- **Who calls**: your team, the AI agent, or the AI first and then your team.
- **The guests**: everyone, one or more groups, guests you hand-pick, or a ready-made suggestion.

A list can be any size. You can have several lists at once, and the same guest can be on more than one list (for example, an invite list and a travel list).

Lists are not the only way to call. For a single guest you don't need a list: open the guest and tap the call button. That call still appears in the guest's chat.

## Where to find Call Guests

- On the wedding **Home** screen, tap the **Call Guests** tile.
- Or go to **More** › **Guest data** › **Calls**.

Both open the **Call lists** screen. If your plan doesn't include Call Guests, the app shows its upgrade sheet instead. Plans are on [weddingkart.co/pricing](https://www.weddingkart.co/pricing).

The **Call lists** screen shows:

- **YOUR CALL LISTS.** Each list shows its name, who is on it (*All guests · 180*, *Group: Bride's Family · 42*, *6 picked guests*), a progress bar, and three counts: **done**, **call back** and **to call**. A line below says who calls (*Team*, *AI first, then team*, *Team · recorded calls*) and names the callers.
- **SUGGESTED LISTS.** Ready-made shortcuts such as **No RSVP yet**, **Wedding invite didn't reach** (*guests, WhatsApp failed*) and **AI couldn't reach** (*guests, last 7 days*). Tap **Make list** to turn one into a normal list. A suggestion only appears when there are guests to put on it.
- **Record calls on your own number.** A banner that explains recorded calls, with **Set up calling number**. You can dismiss it.
- **New call list**, to make a list from scratch.

## Make a call list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap New call list">
    On the **Call lists** screen, tap **New call list**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a purpose">
    Type a **List name**, then under **Pick what this call is for**, choose **Invite to the wedding**, **Confirm RSVP**, **Travel and arrival**, **Food preference** or **Something else**.

    Picking a purpose fills in a draft of **What should the caller say?** and **Questions to ask** for you. Most planners don't write questions from scratch, so edit the draft instead. Each question has an answer type (for example *Yes · Not sure · No*, or *Number*), and the screen reminds you: *Answers save to each guest automatically.* Tap **+ Add question** to add your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who calls">
    Under **Who calls?** choose:
    - **Our team** — *Callers you assign, from their phones or recorded.*
    - **AI agent** — the AI calls every guest on the list. The screen shows the cost per minute before you start.
    - **AI first, then team** — *Team calls the guests the AI couldn't reach.*

    If no calling number is set up yet, a line reads *Your team's calls won't be recorded.* with a **Set up calling number** shortcut. You can ignore it and use **Call from my phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can call this list">
    Under **Who can call this list**, the app shows the team members whose job is **Guest caller**, each with the last digits of their phone number. They are ticked; untick anyone who shouldn't work this list. A guest caller only sees the lists they are ticked on.

    The owner and co-planners don't appear here because they can already call from every list. If nobody has the Guest caller job yet, tap **Add caller**: it opens **Invite to team** with **Guest caller** already chosen, and the person you add comes back ticked. Hired callers on a field staff link get their list from the link itself (see [Hire a caller](/guests/guest-caller-link)). You can also assign callers later. See [Assign callers](#assign-callers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the guests">
    Tap **Continue to Add Guests**. On the **Guests** tab, tick **All guests** at the top, or tick individual guests (search to find them). On the **Groups** tab, tick one or more groups. Then tap the button at the bottom (for example **Add all 180 guests**) to make the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
**All guests** copies everyone who is on the guest list at the moment you make the list. Guests you add to the wedding afterwards are not added automatically. Add them with **Add to a call list…** from the guest list (see below).
</Info>

## Other ways into a call list

### From the guest list: Add to a call list…

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select guests">
    Open **Guests** and tick the checkbox next to the guests you want. **Select All** picks the whole group, not only the rows currently on screen, so this is also the quickest way to call a group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open bulk actions">
    Tap **See bulk actions**. The sheet reads *What do you want to do with N guests?*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add to a call list…">
    It is the first option on the sheet: *Start a new list, or add them to* your existing lists. Pick an existing list, or choose to start a new list pre-filled with these guests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a guest is already on the list you choose, they aren't added twice.

### From an announcement report: Call these N guests

When a WhatsApp announcement didn't reach someone, a phone call is often the next step.

1. Open the announcement's report and go to the **Detailed Report** tab.
2. Open the **Failed** chip. A **Need follow-up** card reads *WhatsApp didn't reach them. Call instead?* and *Makes a call list with these N guests*.
3. Tap **Call these N guests**. The **New call list** form opens pre-filled with exactly those guests and named after the announcement. Finish the form as usual.

The same card appears on the **Delivered** chip (for guests who didn't read it) and, for an RSVP announcement, on **No reply**.

### Suggested lists

On the **Call lists** screen, tap **Make list** on a suggestion. The count on the suggestion matches the guests put on the new list.

### One guest, no list

Tap the call button in the app bar of the guest's page, or in their chat. You get the same *Call from my phone* or *Recorded call* choice. The call is saved to the guest's chat even though there is no list.

## Who calls?

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Our team** | The callers you assign work through the list from their own phones, or with recorded calls. |
| **AI agent** | The AI voice agent calls every guest on the list. This is the same send as an AI voice call from announcements, with the same credits. Guests it can't reach just show as unreached; there is no hand-off to your team. |
| **AI first, then team** | The AI calls everyone first. Guests it couldn't reach, or whose call ended with questions still unanswered, stay on the list for your team. Guests the AI fully finished are marked **Done**. |

With **AI first, then team**, a guest the AI couldn't reach is labelled, for example, *AI couldn't reach · 2 tries*. When the AI got part of the way, your team only sees the questions that are still missing. If a person has already finished a guest and a late AI result arrives afterwards, the person's answers are kept and the guest stays **Done**.

<Note>
Recorded calls and AI calls are placed between **9 am and 9 pm IST**. The AI agent calls are the same ones described in [Voice announcements](/announcements/voice-announcements).
</Note>

## Work a list

Tap a list to open it. The header shows the list's name, who is on it, who calls, and *64 / 180 done* style progress. For an AI list it also says when the AI finished, for example *AI finished yesterday*.

Three tabs sort the guests:

- **To call** — guests nobody has finished with yet.
- **Call back** — guests who asked to be called later, with the time (*Call back 5:30 pm*).
- **Done** — guests whose answers were saved.

Filter chips narrow the list: **All**, **Mine** (guests assigned to you), **AI couldn't reach** and one chip per group. Each guest row shows their name, group, status (*Not called yet*, *AI couldn't reach · 1 try*, *Call back 5:30 pm*), who it is assigned to, and a call button.

Phone numbers are partly hidden on the list. The full number is only used when you tap **Call** on that guest.

### Assign callers

Tap **Assign callers** at the bottom of the list to split guests between callers. You can split the list **evenly**, **by group**, or pick specific guests and give them to one caller. After assigning, each guest has one caller, and **Mine** shows each caller only their own guests.

If a list has callers but a guest isn't assigned to anyone, any caller on the list can take that guest.

## Call a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the call button">
    Tap the call button next to the guest. The sheet reads **Call** and the guest's name, with their number and the list name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to call">
    - **Call from my phone** — *Your own number shows. Not recorded. You fill in the answers when you come back.*
    - **Recorded call** — shows the calling number it will use and says *We ring your phone first, then* the guest, and that the guest hears the call is recorded. It also says *Answers filled for you* and shows the cost per minute. Details are in [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number).

    Tick **Remember my choice for this wedding** to skip this sheet next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the call">
    For **Call from my phone**, your phone's dialer opens with the guest's number. Make the call as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to the app">
    When you return to Weddingkart, the **After the call** form opens. Fill it in as described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no calling number is set up for this wedding, **Recorded call** appears as a prompt instead: *Guests see your business number, and the answers fill in by themselves. Needs a calling number in your business's name (about 5 minutes).* with a **Set up calling number** button. Team members and hired callers who can't set it up see **Ask the planner to set up a calling number** instead of the button.

### Two people, one guest

When someone taps call on a guest, that guest is held for them for **10 minutes**. If a second caller tries to call the same guest in that time, they see a message such as *Priya is calling this guest*. The hold ends when the first caller saves the call, or after 10 minutes.

## After the call (own phone)

Because Weddingkart can't hear a call placed from your own phone, you tell it what happened. The form is titled **After the call** and shows the guest's name and *called from your phone*.

**How did it go?** Pick one:

| Outcome | What happens to the guest on the list |
|---|---|
| **Answered** | Your answers are saved to the guest and the guest moves to **Done**. |
| **No answer** | Nothing is saved. The guest stays on **To call**. |
| **Busy** | Nothing is saved. The guest stays on **To call**. |
| **Call back later** | Pick a time. The guest moves to **Call back** with that time shown. Nothing is saved. |
| **Wrong number** | The guest is marked as unreachable and leaves the **To call** tab. Nothing is saved. |

Answers are only saved when the outcome is **Answered**.

**Questions.** The form lists your list's questions plus anything the guest's record is still missing, for example **RSVP** (*Yes*, *Not sure*, *No*), **Guests coming**, **Food preference** (*Veg*, *Non-veg*) and **Arrival time**. Fill in what the guest told you. Leave the rest blank.

**Voice note.** Instead of typing, tap the microphone, record a **voice note of up to 30 seconds**, and speak naturally (Hindi, English or a mix). Weddingkart turns it into answers and fills in the form, marking them *from your note*. Check each one and fix anything that's wrong. Nothing is saved to the guest until you tap **Save to guest**. If your note is longer than 30 seconds, the app asks you to keep it under 30 seconds; the form stays open so you can try again or type instead.

**Team note.** Add anything the team should remember (for example, a food allergy or a guest's preferred room). The screen reminds you: *Only your team sees this. Never sent to the guest.* A team note can be up to 2,000 characters. It is shown on the call card in chat for your team, and it is never used by the WhatsApp assistant or shared with the guest.

**Save.**
- **Save to guest** saves the call and its answers, then closes the form.
- **Save and call next guest** saves, then opens the next guest on the list.

### If the guest already has a newer answer

Sometimes another source, such as a WhatsApp reply, answers the same question while you're on the call. When that happens the app warns you before overwriting it. By default the newer answer is kept; an answer you typed is only written over it if you choose to replace it.

## Where calls and answers show up

### In the guest's chat

Open **Chat** and then the guest. Calls appear as **call cards** in the same timeline as WhatsApp messages. You'll see cards such as:

- **Human call · Answered · 3m 10s** — who called, from which number, and the answers saved.
- **Human call · No answer**, with *call back 5:30 pm* if one was set.
- **AI call · No answer** — *Nobody picked up*, and the guest is left on the list for the team.
- **Missed call from** the guest's name — a guest called your calling number back and nobody answered (see [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number#when-a-guest-calls-back)).

<Info>
Call cards are not WhatsApp messages. They don't open WhatsApp's 24-hour reply window, they don't count as unread messages, and they never appear under **Needs attention** in the inbox.
</Info>

If someone on your team uses an older version of the app, new call cards don't show for them and nothing breaks. Update the app to see them.

### On the guest's page

The guest page does **not** keep a call history. It holds only what you know now: RSVP, guests coming, arrival time, food preference and so on. Next to each answer a small tag says where it came from: **Human call**, **AI call** or **WhatsApp**. Tap a tag to open that call or message in the chat.

## Who can do what

Call Guests uses the team's normal access. See [Team collaboration](/account/team-collaboration) for how to add people.

| Person | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Wedding owner and co-planners** | Make and edit lists, assign callers, call guests, place recorded calls, and play recordings. Only the owner sets up the calling number. |
| **A team member with the Guest caller job** | Call the guests on the call lists they've been given and save what the guest said. Place recorded calls on the planner's calling number. They can't send announcements or manage payments. |
| **A hired caller on a field staff link** | Call only the guests on the lists you choose, from a web page with no app. See [Hire a caller](/guests/guest-caller-link). |
| **Anyone else on the team** | Can't call guests or assign callers. |

To give a team member the **Guest caller** job, open **Team** and tap **Change access** on their row in the team list. Under **What is their job?** tick **Guest caller** (*Can call guests on the call lists they are given and save what they said*). The preview lists what they will and won't be able to do. Under **Options**, **Show phone numbers** controls whether they see full numbers, and **Play call recordings** controls whether they can hear recorded calls; it is off by default, and when it's off they see answers and summaries only.

<Tip>
Hiring a caller for only a few days? Use a [field staff link](/guests/guest-caller-link) instead of adding them as a team member.
</Tip>

## What if…

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A guest picks up but it's the wrong person?">
    Choose **Wrong number**. The guest leaves the **To call** tab as unreachable and nothing is saved. Fix the number on the guest's page if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The guest asks to be called later?">
    Choose **Call back later** and pick a time. The guest moves to the **Call back** tab with that time, and you can finish them when the time comes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A guest asks us to stop calling?">
    Don't call them again. Call lists are for guests of the wedding, about the wedding. Mark the call **Wrong number** or **No answer** so nobody keeps retrying, and tell the planner so the guest can be left off future lists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I closed the After the call form by mistake?">
    Nothing is saved until you tap **Save to guest**, so reopen the guest on the list, call again if needed, and fill the form in again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AI and a person both reached the same guest?">
    A person's confirmed answers are never overwritten by a late AI result. The guest stays **Done**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my callers need the Weddingkart app?">
    Team members use the app. A caller you hire for a few days can use a field staff link instead: a web page that needs no app. See [Hire a caller](/guests/guest-caller-link).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the guest see my personal number?">
    With **Call from my phone**, yes: the guest sees the number of the phone that placed the call. With **Recorded call**, the guest sees your business's calling number instead. See [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
    The app shows the cost before you start anything that uses credits, for example an AI call or a recorded call. Plans and credit information are on [weddingkart.co/pricing](https://www.weddingkart.co/pricing) and in [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits). Calls from your own phone don't use recorded-call credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I call guests at any time?">
    Recorded calls and AI calls are placed between 9 am and 9 pm IST. Calls from your own phone are placed by your phone, so Weddingkart doesn't stop them, but please keep to the same hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see a guest's full phone number?">
    Numbers are partly hidden for people who haven't been given **Show phone numbers**. The **Call** button still dials the full number when you're allowed to call that guest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my notes visible to guests?">
    No. The team note is only for your team. It is never sent to the guest and never read by the WhatsApp assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see call history on the guest page?">
    Calls live in the guest's chat, next to their WhatsApp messages. The guest page keeps only the answers, each with a tag showing where it came from. Tap the tag to jump to that call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a call list for guests who already replied on WhatsApp?">
    Yes, but you usually won't need to. Use **Suggested lists** such as **No RSVP yet**, or the **Call these N guests** card on an announcement report, to call only the guests who didn't answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I do with the answers a recorded call fills in?">
    You check them. See [Calling number and recorded calls](/guests/calling-number#after-a-recorded-call).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
