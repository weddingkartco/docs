Each wedding you manage in Weddingkart is its own self-contained event — with its own guest list, groups, announcements, and analytics. This page covers how to create and configure a wedding.

## Creating a new wedding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your events list">
    Open the Weddingkart app. After you log in, you land on the list of your weddings and events.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Create Event">
    Tap **Create Event** to open the setup form. If you haven't created anything yet, tap **Continue to create a wedding or Corporate event** instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what you're planning">
    Under **What are you planning?**, pick **A wedding** or **A corporate event** (offsite, conference, awards night). A corporate event has no couple, so it asks for an **Event name** instead of the bride and groom fields below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the wedding details">
    Complete the following fields:

    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Your role** | Your relationship to the wedding: Bride, Groom, Family Member, Event Manager, or Friend. Required. |
    | **Wedding Date** | Pick the date from the calendar. For a multi-day wedding, pick the first day. Required. |
    | **Send messages from** | The WhatsApp number guests receive messages from. Leave it as **Wedding Invite (default)**, or pick your own connected WhatsApp Business number so guests see your business name. This can't be changed after the wedding is created. |
    | **Bride's Name** | Bride's first name (required) and last name (optional). |
    | **Bride's Number** | Optional. |
    | **Groom's Name** | Groom's first name (required) and last name (optional). |
    | **Groom's Number** | Optional. |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create">
    Tap **Create Wedding**. The wedding is created and you're taken to its home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing wedding details

You can update the date and the couple's names and numbers after the wedding has been created.

1. Open the wedding from your list.
2. Tap the **settings** (gear) icon on the wedding's header card.
3. Update the fields you need to change and tap **Save**.

The same screen has a **Delete Wedding** option. The sending number can't be changed here — a wedding keeps the number it was created with.

## Managing multiple weddings

Weddingkart is built for event managers who run several weddings at once. You can create as many weddings as you need from a single account.

To switch between weddings, go back to your list of weddings and tap the one you want to work on. Each wedding is completely separate — guest lists, groups, announcements, and analytics do not overlap between weddings.

## Wedding lifecycle

<Info>
  The wedding date must be today or later when you create a wedding. The wedding stays active up to the wedding date and for 10 days after it. During those 10 days, a banner on the wedding's home screen counts down the days left. After that, the wedding becomes read-only: you can no longer send announcements to guests or change the guest list.
</Info>
