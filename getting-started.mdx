This guide walks you through the core Weddingkart workflow from sign-up to sending your first announcement. By the end, you'll have a wedding set up, a few test guests added, and a real message delivered.

## Where you can use Weddingkart

Weddingkart runs in three places, and they all share one account:

- **Android app** — [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.weddingkart.weddingkart&hl=en)
- **iOS app** — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/weddingkart/id6661023772)
- **Web app** — open [app.weddingkart.co](https://app.weddingkart.co) in any browser on a laptop or desktop

Your account is your mobile number. Log in with the same number on your phone and your laptop and you see the same weddings, guests, and messages. Changes made on one device appear on the others.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.weddingkart.co](https://app.weddingkart.co) (or open the Android or iOS app). On the **Login or sign up** screen, enter your number in **Enter Mobile Number** and tap **Continue**.

    You'll receive a 4-digit one-time password (OTP) by SMS, on WhatsApp, or both. Enter it on the **Enter OTP** screen and tap **Verify**. If this is your first time, your account is created at this point. There's no separate sign-up form.

    No credit card is required to create an account. See [Logging in](#logging-in) below for the details and what to do if the OTP doesn't arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a wedding">
    After logging in, tap **Create Event**. (On a brand-new account, tap **Continue to create a wedding or Corporate event**.)

    Choose **A wedding**, then fill in the details:

    - **Your role** — Bride / Groom / Family Member / Event Manager / Friend
    - **Wedding Date** — for a multi-day wedding, pick the first day
    - **Bride's Name** and **Groom's Name**

    Tap **Create Wedding**. You'll see **🎉  Your Wedding was created!** and land on the wedding's home screen. See [Wedding Setup](/wedding-setup) for every field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a few test guests">
    Open the **Guests** tab. Tap **Add Yourself as Guest** to add your own number. To add 1–2 more, tap **Add Guest** and choose **Add Manually**.

    For each guest, enter their name and phone number. Use your own number or a colleague's for this test so you can see exactly what the guest receives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send your first announcement">
    Open the **Announcement** tab, tap **Add Announcement**, and choose **Send a ready-made message**.

    - Pick a template, such as a wedding invitation, and open it
    - Fill in your message details
    - Attach a PDF, image, or video if you have one (such as a wedding invitation card)
    - Tap **Continue to Add Guests** and select your test guests under **Individuals**
    - Choose **Send Now**

    Your test guests will receive a WhatsApp message within seconds. Check your phone — that's the exact experience your real guests will have.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You've completed the full Weddingkart loop: create a wedding, add guests, and send a message. Now build it out for your real event:

- [Build out your full guest list](/guests/overview) — import from Excel or add guests in bulk
- [Set up RSVPs](/collect/rsvp-management) — collect attendance confirmations with one-tap WhatsApp buttons
- [Explore announcements](/announcements/overview) — schedule messages, use AI to write them, and send to specific groups

<Note>
  The free plan lets you explore every feature in Weddingkart. The only limitation is that you cannot send messages to more than 5 guests at a time. Upgrading to a paid plan removes this limit so you can broadcast to your full guest list.
</Note>

## Logging in

- **Your number and country code.** The number field starts on India (**+91**). If your number is from another country, tap the country code at the start of the field and pick your country before typing, or paste your full number starting with `+` and the country code. On Android, the app may offer the number from your SIM, which you can tap to fill in.
- **How the code is sent.** Weddingkart sends the same 4-digit code by SMS and on WhatsApp. The **Enter OTP** screen tells you which one worked: *"We've sent an OTP to your phone via SMS and WhatsApp."*, *"…via SMS."*, or *"…via WhatsApp."* When WhatsApp was used, a **Go to WhatsApp** link opens the chat where the code arrived.
- **How long it lasts.** An OTP is valid for 10 minutes. Request a new one if it's older than that.
- **Check the number.** The **Enter OTP** screen shows **Verify Mobile Number** followed by the full number, including the country code. If it's wrong, tap **Entered wrong mobile no.?** to go back and fix it.

### If the OTP doesn't arrive

1. Wait for the countdown under the code boxes (**Resend OTP in 00:59**, for example) to finish, then tap **Resend OTP**. You can resend once a minute.
2. Check WhatsApp as well as your SMS inbox. The code can arrive on either.
3. Make sure the country code is right. A number with the wrong country code gets the code sent to a different number. Tap **Entered wrong mobile no.?** and re-enter it.
4. Still stuck? Tap **Facing trouble? Chat with us** on the login screen to message the Weddingkart team on WhatsApp.

### Login messages you might see

| Message | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Please enter a valid phone number** | The number field is empty or contains something other than digits. Check the number and country code. |
| **We couldn't send an OTP to your phone. Please try again.** | Neither SMS nor WhatsApp could be sent this time. Wait a moment and tap **Continue** again. |
| **Please enter a valid OTP** | You tapped **Verify** before entering all 4 digits. |
| **OTP Authentication failed** | The code was wrong or has expired. Tap **Resend OTP** to get a fresh code and enter the new one. |
| **You've requested an OTP too many times. Please wait a few minutes and try again.** | Too many code requests for this number in a short time. Wait a few minutes before requesting another. |
| **Too many login attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again.** | Too many **Verify** attempts in a short time. Wait a few minutes, then try again with a fresh code. |

## What's free

Every account can use every feature without paying:

- **50 free credits per wedding.** Each new wedding starts with 50 credits. One WhatsApp message to an Indian guest uses 1 credit.
- **Up to 5 guests per send.** On a free wedding, one announcement can go to at most 5 guests. If you select more, the app shows *"Since its a free wedding, you are not allowed to send message to more than 5 guests at a time"* and doesn't send.
- **No time limit on trying it out**, other than the wedding's own lifecycle (it becomes read-only 10 days after the wedding date).

Buying a plan for a wedding removes the 5-guest limit and adds credits. Plans are bought per wedding, not per account. See [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits).

## Finding your way around

### Your list of weddings

After you log in you land on your list of weddings under **Your Weddings**. From here:

- Tap a wedding to open it.
- Tap **Create Event** to start a new wedding or corporate event.
- Use the search icon to find a wedding by the bride's, groom's, or event's name.
- Pin the weddings you use most so they stay at the top: long-press a wedding card and tap **Pin Wedding**. This option appears once you have more than 10 weddings, or while the search bar is open.
- Tap the help icon to open **Contact Support**, which offers **WhatsApp Chat**, **Phone Call**, and **Documentation**.
- Open the side menu (drawer) for your account options: **Profile & Settings**, **Activity**, **Refer and Earn**, **Rate Us**, **Your WhatsApp numbers**, **Terms and Conditions**, **Privacy Policy**, **Delete account**, and **Logout**.

### Inside a wedding

The bottom bar has **Home**, **Guests**, **Announcement**, **Chat**, and a fifth tab. On a free wedding the fifth tab is **Wallet**, where you buy a plan. Once the wedding is on a paid plan it becomes **More**, a sheet with everything else:

- **Guest data** — **RSVP**, **ID Cards**, **Travel Tickets**, **Travel & ID**, **Mystery files**, **Export Guest Data**, and **Rooming**
- **Wedding** — **Hampers & Gifts**, **Events**, **Team**, **Candid photos**, and **FAQ**
- **Account & help** — **Wallet**

The wedding's **Home** tab shows, from top to bottom:

- **The wedding card** with the couple's names and date. The gear icon on it opens the wedding's settings, where you can edit details or delete the wedding.
- **SENDING AS** — the WhatsApp number this wedding sends from.
- **A countdown banner**, only after the wedding date has passed. See [Wedding lifecycle](/wedding-setup#wedding-lifecycle).
- **Getting Started** — a 3-step checklist (**Create your wedding**, **Add guests to your list**, **Send your first announcement**) that tracks your progress, such as **2 of 3 done**.
- **Send a Free Sample** (while the wedding has sent very little) — tap **Preview Invite on WhatsApp** or **Send RSVP** to get a sample message on your own WhatsApp.
- **FEATURED** — shortcut tiles such as **Team**, **Candid photos**, **Activity**, and **Call Guests**, plus other tools for your wedding.
- Alerts, upcoming scheduled announcements, today's tasks, and a progress overview.

## Your first-day checklist

Work through these in order for a real wedding:

1. **Decide on the sending number.** If you want guests to see your own business name, [connect your WhatsApp Business number](/account/own-whatsapp-number) *before* you create the wedding. The number can't be changed afterwards.
2. **Create the wedding** with the correct date. For multi-day weddings, use the first day. See [Wedding Setup](/wedding-setup).
3. **Add the events** (Haldi, Sangeet, Reception, and so on) from **Events**, with dates, times, and venues.
4. **Add your team** from **Team** so planners and coordinators can log in and help. See [Team Collaboration](/account/team-collaboration).
5. **Import your guest list** from Excel or add guests by hand. See [Excel Import](/guests/excel-import).
6. **Create groups** such as Brideside, Groomside, or Hotel Guests. See [Guest Groups](/guests/guest-groups).
7. **Send yourself a test** announcement to check the wording and attachment.
8. **Buy a plan** from **Wallet** when you're ready to send to more than 5 guests at a time.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to sign up?">
    No. You sign up with just your mobile number and an OTP. You only pay when you buy a plan for a wedding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a separate sign-up and login?">
    No. The **Login or sign up** screen does both. If your number is new, an account is created the first time you verify the OTP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get the OTP. What should I do?">
    Check both SMS and WhatsApp, since the code can arrive on either. Make sure the country code is right. Then wait for the countdown to finish and tap **Resend OTP**. If it still doesn't come, tap **Facing trouble? Chat with us** on the login screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My number isn't Indian. Can I still log in?">
    Yes. The field starts on +91, but you can tap the country code to pick another country, or paste your number with its `+` country code. Always check the number shown on the **Enter OTP** screen before entering the code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see 'OTP Authentication failed'.">
    The code you entered was wrong or more than 10 minutes old. Tap **Resend OTP** and enter the new code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Weddingkart on my laptop and phone at the same time?">
    Yes. Log in with the same mobile number at [app.weddingkart.co](https://app.weddingkart.co) and in the Android or iOS app. You see the same weddings everywhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do my guests need to install anything?">
    No. Guests receive and reply to everything on WhatsApp. They never need the Weddingkart app or an account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I send to more than 5 guests?">
    Your wedding is on the free plan, which allows at most 5 guests per announcement. Buy a plan for the wedding from **Wallet** to send to everyone. See [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many free credits do I get?">
    Each new wedding starts with 50 free credits. Credits belong to that wedding, and a new wedding gets its own 50.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Wallet? I only see 'More'.">
    On a paid wedding, the fifth tab changes from **Wallet** to **More**. Tap **More** and you'll find **Wallet** under **Account & help**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact Weddingkart support?">
    Tap the help icon at the top of the screen to open **Contact Support**, then choose **WhatsApp Chat** or **Phone Call**. Before you log in, use **Facing trouble? Chat with us** on the login screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
