The referral program lets you earn bonus WhatsApp credits by sharing Weddingkart with other wedding planners and event managers. When someone signs up using your referral link and purchases a wedding plan, you receive credits added directly to your account.

## What you earn

You receive **1,000 bonus credits** for each successful referral — when your referred contact signs up and makes their first purchase. The person you refer also receives **1,000 credits** as a joining bonus when they enter your referral code while signing up (the **Apply & Get Credits** step).

There is no limit to the number of referrals you can make. Every paying referral adds more credits to your account.

<Tip>
The more event managers you refer who become paying customers, the more free messages you accumulate — significantly reducing your per-wedding cost over time.
</Tip>

<Note>
Parts of the **Refer and Earn** screen mention 500 credits. The amount added for each successful referral is 1,000 credits, and the notification you get says the exact number.
</Note>

### When credits are added

| Who | How many | When |
|---|---|---|
| The person who joins with your code | 1,000 credits | Straight away, when they tap **Apply & Get Credits** during sign-up |
| You, the referrer | 1,000 credits | When the person you referred pays for their first wedding plan |

A few rules:

- **The referral pays out once per person.** You earn when the person you referred buys their first plan. Their later weddings and top-ups don't earn you more.
- **The wedding must be one they created.** The reward is triggered when a wedding created by the person you referred is paid for — through Razorpay on the web or Android, or through Apple in-app purchase on iOS.
- **You get a notification** titled **"Referral credits added"**: **"You earned 1000 credits — your referral just signed up. Tap to see your referral balance."** Tapping it opens **Refer and Earn**.
- **You can't use your own code.** Entering it shows **"Can't apply your own referral code."**

### Welcome credits for wedding guests

If someone signs up for Weddingkart for the first time without a referral code, and their phone number is already on a guest list for a wedding, they get **500 free credits**. They see **"Congratulations!"** and **"You have just earned 500 free credits!"**, along with the name of the wedding they're a guest at. These credits work like referral credits: they sit in the personal account and can be transferred to a paid wedding the person creates.

## How to refer someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Referral section">
    Open the Weddingkart app and tap **Refer and Earn** in the drawer menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your referral link">
    Tap **Share Referral Link**, or copy your referral code from the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with a fellow event manager">
    Send the link via WhatsApp, email, or any other channel. A direct message works better than just posting a link publicly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits are added automatically">
    When your contact signs up and pays for a wedding, your 1,000 bonus credits are added to your account automatically and you get a notification. No manual claim required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your referral link also appears in the **Share and Earn** tab of every wedding's [Google Sheet](/account/google-sheets-integration), with a ready-to-send message for each team member.

## Joining with a referral code

The referral code step appears once, when someone signs up for the first time:

1. After logging in, new users see **How did you hear about us?** They can answer or tap **Skip**.
2. The next screen asks for a referral code. If they opened a referral link, the code is filled in and the screen shows **You were referred** and who referred them.
3. They tap **Apply & Get Credits**. When the code works, they see **"Referral applied successfully."** and the credits land in their personal account.
4. They can tap **Continue without referral code** to skip.

If the code can't be applied, they see one of these:

- **"Please enter a valid referral code."** — the code doesn't match any Weddingkart user. Check the spelling, or ask the referrer to share the link instead of typing the code.
- **"Please enter a referral code."** — the field is empty.
- **"Can't apply your own referral code."** — the code belongs to the person entering it.

<Warning>
The referral code screen only appears during first sign-up. If someone skipped it, the app has no other place to add a code later. Share the link before they sign up.
</Warning>

## Using your referral credits

Referral credits are stored in your personal Weddingkart account and are not linked to any wedding by default. Before you can use them, you need to transfer credits from your personal account to a specific wedding.

To use referral credits:

1. Open **Refer and Earn** from the drawer menu to check your **Total Balance**.
2. Tap **Transfer Credits to Wedding**, enter the number of credits, choose the wedding, and tap **Proceed to transfer**.
3. Once transferred, the credits appear in the **Wallet** section of that wedding and can be used for sending WhatsApp announcements.

<Note>
You can only transfer referral credits to weddings that you have created and already paid for. Credits cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another user's account.
</Note>

Referral credits follow the same per-country WhatsApp pricing rules as purchased credits. Referral credits expire 180 days after they are added to your account, so transfer them to a wedding before then.

### Reading the Refer and Earn screen

- **Collected till now** — the credits still sitting in your personal account, including any that have expired. Credits you've already transferred to a wedding aren't counted.
- **Total Balance** (**In your personal Weddingkart account**) — the credits you can transfer right now. Expired credits aren't counted.
- **Transfer Credits to Wedding** — greyed out, with the hint **No credits to transfer**, when your balance is 0.

### The Transfer Credits screen

- **Enter Credits to Transfer** starts at 500. Use the buttons to change it in steps of 500, or type any whole number. The screen shows your **Available credits balance**.
- **Transfer to wedding account** lists the weddings you created, each with its current **Balance**. Weddings you were only added to as a team member don't appear.
- If you pick an unpaid wedding and tap **Proceed to transfer**, you see **Cannot Transfer Credits** — **"You can transfer credits to only a paid wedding."** Buy the wedding's plan first. See [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits).
- If you haven't created any weddings, you see **No weddings yet. You can create new** and a **Create Event** button.
- After a transfer, **Credit Transferred** confirms how many credits moved and to which wedding. Tap **Go to Wedding** to open it.

When you transfer, the credits that expire soonest are used first.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My friend signed up but I didn't get credits">
    You earn when the person you referred **pays for a wedding they created**, not when they sign up. Also check that they entered your code during sign-up — if they tapped **Continue without referral code**, the referral isn't linked to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The referral code wasn't applied">
    If they saw **"Please enter a valid referral code."**, the code was mistyped. Sharing the link with **Share Referral Link** fills the code in for them. The code step only appears on first sign-up, so it can't be added afterwards in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see 'You don't have sufficient credits in your account.'">
    You tried to transfer more than your **Total Balance**. Lower the amount. Remember that expired credits aren't in your balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see 'You need to pay for this wedding before transferring credits.'">
    Referral credits can only go into a paid wedding. Buy the wedding's plan from its **Wallet**, then transfer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see 'You are not the creator of this wedding, can't transfer credits.'">
    You can only transfer your personal credits into weddings you created. If you're a team member on someone else's wedding, ask them to top up instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My balance went down without a transfer">
    Credits expire 180 days after they're added. Expired credits drop out of **Total Balance** but still count in **Collected till now**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much do I earn per referral?">
    1,000 credits for each person who joins with your code and pays for their first wedding. They get 1,000 credits too, as soon as they apply your code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on referrals?">
    No. You earn for every person you refer who pays for a wedding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I earn again when the same person buys another wedding?">
    No. Each referred person earns you credits once, on their first plan purchase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When do referral credits expire?">
    180 days after they're added to your personal account. Transfer them to a paid wedding before then.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do credits expire once they're in a wedding?">
    No. The 180-day expiry applies to credits in your personal account. Once transferred, they're part of the wedding's balance and follow the wedding's rules — see [Payments & Credits](/account/payments-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move credits back out of a wedding?">
    No. Once transferred, credits belong to that wedding. They can't be moved back to your personal account or to another wedding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use referral credits to pay for a plan?">
    No. Referral credits can only be added to a wedding whose plan is already paid for. They can't be used to buy a plan, withdrawn as cash, or given to another user.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer credits to a wedding where I'm only a team member?">
    No. Only weddings you created appear on the **Transfer Credits** screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I apply a referral code after signing up?">
    No. The code step only appears on first sign-up, so share your link before the person creates their account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
