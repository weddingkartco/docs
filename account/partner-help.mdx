Weddingkart is self-serve: you can import guests, send invites and run RSVPs yourself on any plan. If you'd rather have an experienced person help, you can request **partner help**. A Weddingkart partner (an event professional who works with Weddingkart) either sets things up with you or runs guest messaging for you.

## The two options

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Assisted** | A partner sets things up with you on a call: guest list import, your first invite sent, and a WhatsApp setup check. Template creation is not included. |
| **Managed** | A partner runs guest messaging for your wedding for you: sets up your guest list, sends your invitations, and keeps RSVP and travel updates going through the wedding, coordinated with you on WhatsApp in English or Hindi. Template creation and your Weddingkart plan and credits are not included. |

The same screen also offers help with writing WhatsApp templates and with WhatsApp Business setup. It shows each option's current price. Prices are also on [weddingkart.co/pricing](https://www.weddingkart.co/pricing).

Event managers usually choose **Assisted**; couples usually choose **Managed**.

<Note>
Partner help is the partner's own service. **You pay the partner directly** (for example by UPI), not Weddingkart, and the partner sends their own invoice. It is separate from your Weddingkart plan and credits, which you still buy in the app.
</Note>

## How to request it

1. Open your wedding in the Weddingkart app.
2. Tap the **Partner help** tile in the Features section (partner help is also listed on the **Wallet** screen).
3. Read the two options and tap **Message us on WhatsApp**. (Changed your mind? Tap **Keep doing it yourself**.)
4. WhatsApp opens a chat with Weddingkart (+91 92176 10045), with a short message already filled in. Tap **Send**.
5. Our team replies on WhatsApp within a few hours and assigns a partner to your wedding.

Back in the app, the Partner help screen shows **Requested on** and the date. Tap **Open chat again** to return to the WhatsApp conversation.

## Good to know

- Only the wedding's admins and members can request partner help; guests never see it.
- Managed is not offered in the last 14 days before the wedding, and Assisted not in the last 2 days, because there isn't enough time to deliver it well. If you already requested it, you can still open the chat.
- If WhatsApp doesn't open on your device, the app shows our number so you can message us directly.
- Once you've paid the partner, the Partner help screen shows that your partner help is active.
