---
title: "Appearance (dark mode)"
description: Show Weddingkart in light or dark colours, or follow your phone's setting
---

Weddingkart can show every screen in light or dark colours. By default it follows your phone or browser: when your device is in dark mode, Weddingkart is dark too. You can also force it to stay light or stay dark.

## Change the appearance

1. Open the menu (**☰**) on the **Your Weddings** screen.
2. Tap **Appearance**.
3. Pick one of the three options. The sheet says *Choose how Weddingkart looks on this device.*

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Same as phone** | The default. Weddingkart is dark when your phone, tablet or browser is set to dark mode, and light otherwise. It switches by itself when your device switches, for example at night if your phone changes to dark mode automatically. |
| **Light** | Always light, even when your device is in dark mode. |
| **Dark** | Always dark, even when your device is in light mode. |

You can also reach the same picker from the **Team Inbox**: tap **⋮** (**More options**) at the top right and choose **Appearance**.

<Note>
Your choice is saved **on this device and browser only**. It doesn't follow your account, so a second phone, a laptop or another browser starts on **Same as phone** until you change it there too. Each team member picks their own appearance; it never changes what anyone else sees.
</Note>

## What dark mode covers

Dark mode covers the whole app: your weddings list, every wedding's **Home**, **Guests**, **Announcement**, **Chat** and their screens, and the sheets and pop-ups opened from them.

A few screens always stay light, because they show a web page or a printed document rather than an app screen:

- **Wallet** and the payment screens (plans, credit packs and the payment step).
- The Facebook / Meta steps for **connecting your own WhatsApp number**.
- The **guest experience** page.
- PDFs and documents you open from a chat, which keep their white pages.

WhatsApp message **previews** keep WhatsApp's own look (green bubbles, the WhatsApp header) so you see roughly what a guest will see.

## Weddings and corporate events

Weddings use Weddingkart's maroon and blush colours. **Corporate events** use navy blue on warm grey, so you can tell at a glance which kind of event you're in. The colours change when you open an event and go back to maroon when you return to **Your Weddings**. Both kinds of event support light and dark.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the dark mode switch?">
    Open the menu (**☰**) on **Your Weddings** and tap **Appearance**, or tap **⋮** on the **Team Inbox** and choose **Appearance**. Pick **Dark** to always use dark colours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app went dark by itself">
    Weddingkart follows your device by default (**Same as phone**). Your phone or browser switched to dark mode, often on a schedule at night. Pick **Light** under **Appearance** to keep Weddingkart light.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I chose Dark but some screens are still light">
    **Wallet**, the payment screens, the Facebook steps for connecting your own WhatsApp number, the guest experience page and opened PDFs always stay light. They show a web page or a document that can't change colour.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My other phone or my laptop is still light">
    The setting is saved per device and browser. Open **Appearance** on that device too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does dark mode change what my guests see?">
    No. It only changes how Weddingkart looks for you on this device. Guests still receive normal WhatsApp messages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my corporate event blue?">
    Corporate events use a navy colour scheme so they look different from weddings. When you go back to **Your Weddings**, the app returns to Weddingkart's maroon.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
